# Image quality and color

- Gain, contrast, saturation, and sharpen are in the *Image* group of *Advanced video settings* [2024-03], [2024-09].
- To reduce brightness, lower *Gain* [2024-03].
- There is no denoising, and sharpen is the only detail control [2023-09], [2024-09].
- *Resolution* (supersampling) makes sharpening more effective. See [Formats and performance](../playing-videos/formats-and-performance.md#resolution-setting) [2024-11].
- On PC, post-processing also affects the menu [2021-06].

To make your image settings the default for new videos, see [Presets](presets.md).
