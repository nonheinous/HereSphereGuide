# Adjusting the image

Most videos look right with default settings. Adjust only when something looks wrong: warped lines, people who seem giant or tiny, eyestrain, or double vision. These settings live in *Advanced video settings* and are saved per video [2021-07].

To adjust a number box, hover over it, hold grip, and move the thumbstick. To see the image while adjusting, hide the menu with grip held; the thumbstick still changes the setting [2021-07].

## Which setting fixes what

| Symptom | Try first | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straight lines look warped or curved | FOV and lens type ([Projection](projection-lens-fov.md)) | Stitch shift Y, stitch scale Y |
| Everything looks too big or too small | Camera stereo alignment right (camera IPD) | Stereo alignment yaw, stitch shift X |
| Depth readings are wrong | Stitch shift X | Stereo alignment yaw |
| Double vision or eyestrain looking straight ahead | Stitch shift Y | Stitch scale Y near top and bottom edges |
| Bottom of the image feels too far away | Stitch shift X until the bottom looks right | Stereo alignment yaw to push the center back, or roll [2025-06] |
| Image shifts when turning your head | Autofocus min/max distance or distance override ([Autofocus](autofocus.md)) | Keep head rotation near 45° and move your eyes [2021-07] |
| Video starts too close | Zoom out, then save the Origin preset as default ([Presets](presets.md)) [2024-04] | |
| Borders slanted at top and bottom | Usually normal: a lens narrower than 180° padded to fit [2021-05] | |
