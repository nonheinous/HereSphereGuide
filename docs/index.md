# HereSphere Community Guide

An unofficial guide to the HereSphere VR video player, built from the developer's answers in the HereSphere Discord `#instructions` channel (May 2020 – September 2026).

!!! warning "Unofficial and in progress"
    This guide is maintained by the community and is not endorsed by the HereSphere developer. Menus move between releases, so verify instructions in your version of the app. Found something wrong? Use the pencil icon at the top of any page to suggest a fix.

## Platforms

HereSphere runs on three platforms. When an instruction applies to only one, it's labeled **Quest**, **PC**, or **AVP**.

| Platform | Where it runs |
| --- | --- |
| Quest | Standalone on Meta Quest headsets (Meta store or sideloaded from itch.io) |
| PC | Windows PC through SteamVR, streamed to a headset |
| AVP | Apple Vision Pro |

## How to read dates

Dates in brackets, like [2025-11], show when the developer last gave that answer. Anything dated before 2024 may describe menus that have since moved.
